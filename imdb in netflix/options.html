See IMDb ratings on every Netflix title

This extension adds IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metascore ratings to Netflix title cards. Hover over a badge to see the poster, plot and cast. A free API key is required to retrieve ratings.

Get a free API key

It takes about two minutes and is free. The free OMDb plan includes 1,000 requests per day. Ratings are cached for a week to reduce requests.

  1. 1

    Open the OMDb signup page

    OMDb provides the ratings. Open omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx in a new tab.

    omdbapi.com
    API Key
  2. 2

    Choose FREE and enter your email address

    Choose the free plan (1,000 requests per day), enter your details and press Submit. You can enter “Personal” in the “Use” field.

Optional trailers

Enable trailers in the hover info card

Trailers are off by default. Add your own free TMDb API key and enable the switch to use this feature. Without both, no trailer requests are made.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Troubleshooting

Diagnosis: why aren’t ratings showing?

Test the connection and look up a title (use the title and year as shown on Netflix). Share the result if it doesn’t work.


  

  
Ratings incorrect or outdated?  and refresh Netflix.

  
Seeing “–” for a title? Some titles may not be found, especially when Netflix uses a localized title. The extension prefers showing no score over displaying a rating for the wrong movie or series.
